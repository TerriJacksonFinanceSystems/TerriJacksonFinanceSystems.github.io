Monthly FP&A Process

Horizontal functional swimlane supporting the HTML FP&A dashboard prototype.

← PortfolioCase studyRACIDashboard
Purpose: turn approved cost-centre actuals into a reconciled, decision-focused management story. The dashboard performs technical checks and calculations; it does not validate the accounting source or approve decisions.

Horizontal functional swimlanes Monthly cycle moves left to right Human review and approval remain explicit

General Ledger / R2R Team1 Complete Close

2 Release Actuals

HTML FP&A Dashboard6 Technical Input Gate
Require Department, Cost Category, Cost 2026 and Cost 2025; parse valid numeric rows and reject an unusable file.		7 Calculate and Render
Calculate selected-view costs, YoY £/% movement and headcount metrics; render KPIs, charts, waterfall, heatmap, GL drivers and rule-based narrative.		Reactive Dashboard Support
As selections change, filter and sort the loaded data, recalculate the selected view and refresh charts and rule-based presenter narrative.
Cost-centre Budget Holder10 Explain Material Drivers
Evidence material movements: timing, volume, price, headcount, scope and one-offs.		12 Agree Accountable Actions
Agree actions, named owners and due dates with FP&A; confirm what changes before the next review.
CFO / Finance Leadership14 Review and Decide
Challenge priorities, decide or approve actions, and confirm ownership and escalation.
